Client privacy & discretion

Privacy deserves consideration.

How journey details are used, what to share and how to discuss privacy preferences with BostonLuxLimos.

Share only what your journey needs.

Our quote process asks for contact and itinerary details so we can respond, review availability and coordinate transportation. Please do not send payment-card numbers, passport details, passwords or sensitive personal documents through the quote form.

How itinerary information is used.

Information submitted through our Google quote form is recorded in a private response spreadsheet for reservations administration. Relevant details may be shared with personnel or transportation providers needed to arrange your requested journey.

Discuss discretion before you travel.

If your itinerary involves a sensitive destination, a guest travelling under an organization’s arrangements, or a particular communication preference, speak with reservations before confirming. Tell us who should receive updates and the safest way to contact you. Specific requests must be reviewed and agreed in advance.

Comfort for your conversations.

A private vehicle gives you space away from a shared commute. For highly sensitive calls, consider what can be heard by others in the vehicle. Transportation should not be treated as a soundproof or legally privileged setting.

Your information, your questions.

Ask about access, correction or deletion of information by contacting reservations. Some records may need to be retained for operational or legal requirements. A quote inquiry does not itself subscribe you to promotional messages.
