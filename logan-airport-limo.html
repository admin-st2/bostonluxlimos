Logan Airport
Private Car Service.

Travel to and from Boston Logan International Airport with private, prearranged chauffeur transportation. We serve travelers connecting Boston, Cambridge and communities throughout New England.

Request a Personalized Quote

Arriving at Logan Airport

Tell us your airline, flight number, arrival date, passenger count and luggage needs. We will discuss pickup meeting points, timing and any applicable waiting charges before you confirm a reservation. Airport pickup locations depend on terminal and current airport rules.

Departing from Boston

For departures, share the pickup address, flight departure time and number of travelers. We will coordinate a proposed pickup time for your quote and confirm scheduling when you book.

Boston and beyond

Common travel destinations include Downtown Boston, Back Bay, Seaport, Cambridge, Brookline, Newton and Providence. Longer-distance New England trips are quoted individually.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide transfers to and from all Logan terminals?

Request a quote with your airline and terminal. We will confirm feasible pickup arrangements and availability based on the applicable airport rules.

Can I request a round trip?

Yes. Provide both dates and times to receive a quote for the outbound and return transfers.

Can you accommodate groups and luggage?

Tell us the passenger and luggage counts. Vehicle suitability and availability will be confirmed with your quote.

Every journey deserves distinction.

Provide your pickup, destination, date, passenger count and any special requests. BostonLuxLimos will review your quote request. An inquiry does not confirm availability or a booking.
