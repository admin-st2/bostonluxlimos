## Review each introduction
Record follow-up status in private business records: New, Reviewing, Awaiting rates, Qualification review, Discussion, Approved by owner, Declined. Include last contact, next action, responsible person, rate-sheet version/currency and agreed operating contact. Keep terms and any client data private.

Review company website, service areas, dispatch hours/time zone, vehicle options, licensing/insurance overview, chauffeur standards, subcontracting, privacy and accessibility. Ask for appropriate evidence through a secure follow-up channel, not the public form.

Compare affiliate net versus retail rates, currency, minimums, inclusions, tolls/parking, airport fees, gratuity, wait time, extra stops, peak/after-hours fees, taxes, deposits, cancellation and no-show terms, quote validity, invoicing and settlement. Do not accept terms or commercial commitments without owner approval.

Agree who owns the booking conversation, quotes and confirms, charges the client, manages dispatch, handles flight changes and escalations, and resolves complaints. Discuss client relationship boundaries, permitted branding and authorized data sharing before any trip.
