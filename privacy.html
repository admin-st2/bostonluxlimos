Quote requests

BostonLuxLimos, operated by STRUT2 LLC, uses the name, email address, phone number and journey details you submit to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes and coordinate requested transportation.

Google Forms

Our quote form is hosted by Google. Submitted responses are recorded in a private Google Sheet used for reservations administration. Google's own privacy practices apply to its service. Do not submit payment-card numbers, passwords, passport details or sensitive documents.

Communications & access

Submitting a quote request allows us to respond about that request. It does not by itself subscribe you to promotional messages. We may share relevant itinerary details with personnel or transportation providers needed to arrange your requested service.

Questions or requests

To ask about information you have provided or request a correction or deletion, email reservations@bostonluxlimos.com. Some records may need to be retained for operational or legal requirements.
